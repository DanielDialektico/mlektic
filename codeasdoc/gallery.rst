Gallery
-------

The canonical interactive gallery is the notebook suite, because equations,
sliders, lesson stages, and three-dimensional rotation cannot be evaluated from
a screenshot alone.

* ``qa_00_smoke_matrix.ipynb`` — every tabular dimensional route;
* ``qa_01_linear.ipynb`` — linear models, preprocessing, and predictions;
* ``qa_02_logistic.ipynb`` — binary and multiclass variants;
* ``qa_03_neural.ipynb`` — architecture, graph, training, weights, and forward pass;
* ``qa_04_motion.ipynb`` — playback and temporal semantics;
* ``qa_05_visual_system.ipynb`` — every theme, format, density, and size;
* ``qa_06_data_edges.ipynb`` — numerical and data-regime edge cases.
* ``qa_07_hyperparameters.ipynb`` — estimator regularization, optimization,
  weighting, neural topology, task, and loss variants.

Each cell names what a reviewer should inspect and displays one real public-API
figure. Stored outputs are deliberately cleared to keep the repository small and
avoid stale visual evidence. Execute locally or use the scheduled full-notebook
CI artifact. Representative case IDs are ``SMOKE-LR-2D``,
``SMOKE-LOG-MULTI-2D``, and ``NN-GRAPH-EXACT``.
